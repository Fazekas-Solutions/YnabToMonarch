Where do I find my YNAB export?

  1. 1

    Sign in to YNAB

    Visit YNAB.com and log into your account.

  2. 2

    Export your plan

    Click your name in the top-left corner, then Export Plan. This downloads a .zip with all your data.

  3. 3

    Upload it here

    Drag the .zip onto the upload area, or click to browse for it. Tap the upload area and choose your .zip file.

  4. 4

    Review & map

    Choose which accounts to bring over and how they map into Monarch.

  5. 5

    Import to Monarch

    Everything moves over in one pass: transactions and balances.
